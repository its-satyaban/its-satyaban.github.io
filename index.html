U05 · WORK

Selected infrastructure work

Five builds, each written as: what was broken, what I did, what changed.

Remote access architecture

Secure remote access for a distributed workforce

Staff across several geographically separated sites needed to reach internal systems without exposing them to the open internet, and the existing access path was generating recurring incidents.

I designed and now operate the FortiClient VPN infrastructure using both SSL and IPsec tunnels, sized for concurrent load and split by site. Access is tied to Active Directory identity, and tunnel policy is enforced at the FortiGate rather than on the client.

Supports 100+ concurrent users across multiple sites with access-related incidents down sharply.

FortiGate 100FSSL VPNIPsecFortiClient EMSActive Directory
Endpoint management · MD-102

Managed endpoint lifecycle and threat visibility

Devices were being onboarded inconsistently, and there was no enforced way to stop sensitive customer data leaving the organisation.

I administer Microsoft Intune (MDM/MAM) for onboarding, compliance policy, conditional access and remote wipe, and work in the Microsoft Defender portal for threat detection, incident triage and security posture across the estate. Bitdefender GravityZone handles real-time response on the endpoint, and Sophos DLP rules guard against unauthorised exfiltration.

Every device enrolled, compliant and remotely recoverable; threats surfaced and triaged from a single console.

Intune MDM/MAMMicrosoft DefenderConditional accessBitdefender GravityZoneSophos DLPTrueNAS
Compliance & audit

SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 audit readiness

The organisation needed to demonstrate to external auditors that its IT controls were not just written down but actually operating — the part most teams fail on.

I ran evidence collection across access control, patching, logging and endpoint protection, identified where controls had gaps in practice, remediated them, and coordinated directly with the external auditors through the review. I also authored the SOPs, network runbooks, security policies and incident response playbooks that keep the controls repeatable after the audit ends.

Controls evidenced and gaps closed across both frameworks; governance documentation now standard operating practice.

Fleet migration

Windows 11 rollout across 150+ endpoints

A 150-seat call centre had to move to Windows 11 without losing a working day or a single user's data — in an environment where idle agents cost money by the minute.

I built the standard image, resolved driver compatibility ahead of rollout, sequenced the migration by team, and validated each machine post-deployment before handing it back.

150+ endpoints migrated with zero data loss and no extended outage to floor operations.

Network & perimeter

Segmented LAN/WLAN with next-gen perimeter defence

A flat network means one compromised endpoint reaches everything. The estate also had to stay reachable for 200+ users with no tolerance for connectivity gaps.

I run the LAN and wireless layer on Cisco CBS350 managed switches, Aironet 1850 wireless and TP-Link PoE, with traffic separated by VLAN policy and inspected at FortiGate 100F/80F firewalls for threats and web filtering at the perimeter.

99.5%+ uptime maintained across business-critical systems with segmentation limiting lateral movement.
